Complementary product

Complementary to the traditional CV
Concept

The CV that
objectifies behavior.

The traditional CV describes a career path. It says nothing — or almost nothing — about how a candidate operates, their deeper motivations, or their preferred modes of interaction.

The Major Exchanges Roder5 complements the traditional CV with an objective mapping of behavioral and motivational skills, derived from the Major Exchanges methodology.

Content

What Roder 5
reveals.

01

Dimensions

Visualization across 8 dimensions: drive, relationships, character, reflection, organization, sales, management, emotions.

02

Motivations

Identification of personal motivational drivers and the professional environments in which the person thrives.

03

Complementarities

Team profiles needed for optimal collaboration. Management styles that work best.

04

Points of attention

Areas that require more attention in certain contexts, to anticipate and ensure the quality of professional commitments.

05

Synthesis

A clear and actionable summary page, designed to be shared with a recruiter or a manager.
