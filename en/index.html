04 — Methodology

An analysis
across five levels.

From Integration to Drive, Major Exchanges offers a structured approach across five levels articulated around a central engine — a multi-dimensional reading rare on the HR behavioral assessment market.

I.

Integration

How the person is anchored in their professional environment — the ability to fit within a framework, rules and relationships.

II.

Motivation

The deep levers that sustain engagement — the sources of energy that guide action over time.

III.

Organization

The structuring of action: method, priorities, time management, alignment of means and mission objectives.

IV.

Predisposition

Natural dispositions, inclinations — the areas where the person naturally deploys their capacities.

V.

Drive

The central engine. The force that sets the other four levels in motion and gives the whole its coherence.
