Expert positioning

A tool that
enhances your expertise.

Major Exchanges was designed for HR practitioners — HR Directors, consultants, coaches, firms — who want to enrich their practice without giving up what makes them valuable: judgment, listening, the human reading.

The tool gives the expert a framework to structure the interview, a set of questions and self-positioning exercises, and a matrix of 48 requirements to deliver their conclusions to the final decision-maker. The methodology, historically registered under the name Roder5, is today marketed under the Major Exchanges brand.

The practitioner at the center.

The guide structures the interview without confining it. The expert retains control over pacing, follow-ups, and interpretive perspective — the tool provides the grid and the cross-referencing, not the decision.

It is this articulation — rigorous method, human judgment — that distinguishes Major Exchanges from the automated questionnaires on the market.

What you gain

Five levels of study — integration, motivation, organization, predisposition and drive. Angles that conventional tools do not cover.

Lever 02
