Graphic & written synthesis

Three stages,
one construction.

Each study unfolds in three complementary stages: online questionnaire completion, in-person or remote interview with the candidate, and study of the application through cross-referencing of information.

Online questionnaire
15 min

Guided questionnaire completion at the candidate's own pace, on any device. AI pre-analysis generated automatically.

Guided interview · video call
1 h

Interview conducted by your recruitment staff (or by a Major Exchanges advisor upon request) to strengthen the relationship and cross-reference information.

Delivery & debrief
1 h

Completion of the personality study and delivery of the full file as a high-definition PDF.
