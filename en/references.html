Case studies

Four use cases,
four perspectives.

Occitanie Chamber of Commerce and Industry

We use the Major Exchanges-Roder5 expert system as an interactive support for conducting personality and motivation studies of the staff we recruit.

To this end, Major Exchanges enables:

  • The upstream analysis, facilitated by the tool, which makes it possible to define the requirements of the position to be filled.
  • The completion of questionnaire grids by candidates, whenever they wish within a given period, via a dedicated website.
  • A report on the behavioral, managerial and motivational qualities of the candidates.

Major Exchanges significantly improves the approach to and analysis of candidates' behavioral qualities and motivations. It enables a more objective exchange of information, by facilitating and encouraging its expression.

Beyond this, Major Exchanges strengthens the quality of integration and engagement of new employees. The methodology and the psychometric quality of the expert system have been validated by the CCI, which has been using them for many years.

Didier Berni
Human Resources Director, CCI Occitanie
French Atomic Energy Commission

We use the Major Exchanges expert system and its questionnaire grids in the following ways:

  • Completion of questionnaire grids by our candidates through the company's website.
  • A telephone interview with candidates, conducted by the firm's consultants.
  • Generation of an automated preliminary study of candidates' personality, based on the requirements of the position.
  • When necessary, a telephone and/or face-to-face interview with candidates.
  • The subsequent production of a comprehensive study.

The psychometric qualities of this assessment methodology (« Inter Projective Methodology ») have been validated by our human resources department on more than 350 recruitments.

Maxime Guillaume
Head of HR services, CEA Marcoule
Philippe Bigeon
Head of employment services, CEA Marcoule
SUEZ Environnement

The Major Exchanges expert system has supported SUEZ Environnement in developing an approach based, in particular, on a methodology and computerized questionnaires for assessing and monitoring collective and individual potential, marketed and operated by the company.

The initiative developed in partnership with the company focuses on measuring the effectiveness of the assimilation of business, behavioral and managerial know-how components, at both the individual level and within homogeneous populations (by Department, Service, etc.).

These various methodological elements help build a tailored Know-How Transfer policy: adapting the managerial strategy of international experts to the local context, and optimizing the support provided to local managers based on the profiles encountered.

This approach reinforces the SUEZ Environnement Group's expertise in its strategic support missions within the framework of emerging Management Contracts — dynamic management of a public service's transformation, sustainable modernization, and progressive empowerment of local teams.

Jean-Marc Jahn
General Manager, SEAAL
Institute for Radiological Protection and Nuclear Safety

The Major Exchanges assessment questionnaires are used at IRSN for its recruitment and internal mobility processes:

  • Online completion of the questionnaires by our candidates and employees.
  • A report on behavioral, managerial and motivational qualities.
  • A telephone interview for recruitments requiring a more in-depth study.

The methodology of the tools and their psychometric quality have been validated at IRSN, which has been using them since 2005. This is based on experience covering more than 800 recruitments.

Patricia de la Morlais
Human Resources Department, IRSN
